## Speech To Text

Begin a voice recording.

[![](../../../../media/feature_overview/chat_interface/user/stt_button.jpeg)](../../../../media/feature_overview/chat_interface/user/stt_button.jpeg)

A timeline indicates how long the recording has lasted.

[![](../../../../media/feature_overview/chat_interface/user/stt_record.jpeg)](../../../../media/feature_overview/chat_interface/user/stt_record.jpeg)

To complete the recording and transcribe the audio as a prompt select the check mark.

[![](../../../../media/feature_overview/chat_interface/user/stt_submit.jpeg)](../../../../media/feature_overview/chat_interface/user/stt_submit.jpeg)

The recorded audio will be displayed as input which can be altered or directly sent to the model.

[![](../../../../media/feature_overview/chat_interface/user/stt_message.jpeg)](../../../../media/feature_overview/chat_interface/user/stt_message.jpeg)
