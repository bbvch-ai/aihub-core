# 🛡️ The Swiss AI-Hub Basic Tier: Your Secure Gateway to AI

::: info Foundation Layer
The Basic Tier is the foundational entry point into the Swiss AI-Hub. It is designed to empower your entire organization with direct, secure, and controlled access to the raw power of advanced Large Language Models (LLMs). This tier serves as a sandbox for learning and discovery, allowing your teams to build AI literacy and explore potential use cases in a safe, Swiss-hosted environment.
:::

## 💬 A World-Class Chat Experience: More Than Just a Text Box

Beyond integrations, the Swiss AI-Hub provides its own beautiful, modern, and feature-rich chat interface. It's an enterprise-grade platform designed for productivity, collaboration, and deep interaction with AI.

### 📁 Organize Your Work, Collaborate with Your Team

* **Project-Based Folders:** Organize your chats into distinct folders, each of which can have its own system prompt and associated knowledge. This is perfect for managing different projects, clients, or initiatives without context bleed.
* **Collaborative, AI-Enhanced Notes:** Create notes, record meeting audio, and let the AI instantly transcribe, summarize, or refine your text. You can collaborate with multiple users in real-time, manage checklists, and even "chat" directly with your notes to ask questions or have the AI help you write.
* **Secure Chat Sharing:** Share conversations with colleagues via a secure link, making it easy to transfer knowledge or get feedback on an AI-generated result.

### 🎤 Interact Your Way: Text, Voice, and Visuals

* **Hands-Free Voice Calls:** Engage in natural, hands-free voice conversations with the AI. You can even interrupt the AI by simply tapping the screen, making for a fluid and intuitive dialogue.
* **Rich File and Media Support:** Upload a wide range of documents (**PDFs**, **Word**, **PowerPoint**, **OpenDocument Text**) for the AI to analyze, summarize, or answer questions about. Paste YouTube links for instant video summaries or record audio directly in the interface.
* **Built-in Image Generation:** Generate images directly within the chat interface using integrated models like DALL-E or Stable Diffusion.
* **Rich Text and Diagrams:** Format your messages using a full-featured rich text editor and render complex information visually with support for **Mermaid diagrams**.

### 🚀 Boost Your Productivity

* **Powerful Prompting Tools:** Create reusable prompt templates with custom variables, which will automatically generate an input form for you to fill out. Use the Tab key for intelligent prompt auto-completion and get smart "Follow Up" suggestions after each AI response to keep the momentum going.
* **Effortless Navigation and Search:** Instantly find any chat, model, or document using a powerful universal search (Ctrl+K). Pin your most important chats for quick access and use the interactive chat overview to visualize and navigate complex conversation flows.
* **Full Export Capabilities:** Download your chats as **PDF**, **JSON**, or copy responses with full formatting preserved, making it easy to document and share your work.

## 💼 AI Where You Work: Seamless Integration into Your Daily Tools

We believe that AI should adapt to your workflow, not the other way around. As a result, in addition to the chat interface, the Base Tier brings the power of advanced language models directly into the collaboration tools your teams use every day, such as Microsoft Teams, Slack, and email.
Rather than opening a separate AI app, users can chat with AI right inside the tools they already use for work - like Slack, Teams, or other messaging platforms. This also allows interacting in the same conversation with coworkers and with AI.

**The Value for You:**
* **Boosted Productivity:** Get answers, draft responses, and summarize information without ever leaving your primary workspace. This eliminates constant context-switching and keeps your focus sharp.
* **Seamless Adoption:** By integrating AI into familiar environments, the learning curve is minimized. Employees can start benefiting from AI assistance immediately, leading to faster adoption and a quicker realization of value.
* **Centralized and Secure:** All interactions, whether in Teams or our web interface, are managed through the central, secure hub, ensuring that your company's data and conversations are protected under a unified governance model.

## 🔐 The Foundation of Trust and Sovereignty

::: danger Swiss Values Foundation
The Basic Tier is built upon the core Swiss values of privacy, security, and independence. This provides a level of trust that is essential when deploying AI across an organization.
:::

* **Full Data Sovereignty:** The Swiss AI-Hub can be fully self-hosted on your own infrastructure or in a Swiss cloud. This guarantees that your data, conversations, and models remain under your control, in Switzerland, completely independent of American or other foreign corporations and their jurisdictions.
* **Security and Granular Control:** The platform is secure by design. Admins have granular control over user permissions, including who can access which models or features like file uploads. Detailed audit logs track important events like failed login attempts, and API key access can be tightly restricted to specific endpoints.
* **A Transparent, Monitorable System:** We believe in demystifying AI. The platform is designed to be transparent. Managers can monitor usage and performance, and the underlying architecture ensures that AI agents are built from testable, auditable steps, not inscrutable black boxes.
