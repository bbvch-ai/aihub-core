# Contributing to the Swiss AI-Hub

First off, thank you for considering contributing! We are thrilled to have you here. This project is a community-driven
effort, and every contribution - from fixing a typo to proposing a new sovereign AI strategy - is incredibly valuable.

This document is your starting guide. It will help you find the best way to make an impact and direct you to the
resources you need.

## Code of Conduct

We enforce a Code of Conduct to ensure our community is welcoming and inclusive for everyone. Please take a moment to
read it before you get started.

## 💬 Join Our Community: The First Step

Before you go any further, we highly recommend you **[join our Discord Server](https://discord.gg/wArT8zDB)**!

This is the central hub of our community. We make a key distinction between our community platforms:

- **👾 Discord is for...**

  - Getting help running the platform.
  - Asking questions about using the SDK to build agents, pipelines, or processes.
  - Helping other users and sharing what you've built.
  - General community discussion and hanging out.

- **🐙 GitHub is for...**

  - Discussing the *development and building* of the platform itself.
  - Reporting bugs (Issues).
  - Proposing new features (Discussions).
  - Submitting code (Pull Requests).

## 🚀 Ways to Contribute

There are many ways to support the Swiss AI-Hub, and many of them don't require writing a single line of code.

- **[Contribute Code](#-contributing-code-the-workflow)**: Help us build the next generation of the platform.
- **[Improve Documentation](#-other-ways-to-contribute)**: Fix typos, clarify complex topics, or write new guides in the
  `docs` folder.
- **[Help the Community](#-join-our-community-the-first-step)**: Join our Discord, answer questions, and help us build a
  vibrant and supportive environment.
- **[Improve Branding & Marketing](#-other-ways-to-contribute)**: Help us refine our message on our website or in our
  `slidev`-based marketing materials.
- **[Be an Advocate](#-other-ways-to-contribute)**: Propose a sovereign AI strategy at your company or showcase the
  Swiss AI-Hub to your colleagues.

## 🧑‍💻 Contributing Code: The Workflow

We have a structured workflow to ensure all contributions are high-quality and aligned with our goals. Please follow
these steps.

### Step 1: Find or Propose a Task

**We strongly discourage "floating" Pull Requests** (PRs that are not connected to an existing issue or discussion). All
work should be tracked.

- **To Fix a Bug:** First, **[create a new GitHub issue](https://github.com/bbvch-ai/swiss-ai-hub/issues/new)** to
  describe the bug.
- **To Propose a Feature:** First,
  **[start a new GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/bbvch-ai/swiss-ai-hub/discussions)** to outline your idea.
- **To Find Work:** The best way is to go to our
  **[GitHub Project Roadmap](https://github.com/orgs/bbvch-ai/projects/13)**. Look for small (XSS, XS or S) features in
  future months that are not yet assigned.

### Step 2: Discuss Your Implementation

**This is a crucial step.** Before you write any code, please outline your implementation approach in the associated
Issue or Discussion. This allows the core team to provide guidance, prevent duplicate work, and ensure your solution
aligns with the project's architecture *before* you invest a lot of time.

### Step 3: Set Up Your Environment

We do not include the full setup instructions here, as our documentation is the single source of truth.

- **To Build with the SDK:** If you are building a new agent, pipeline, or process, follow the
  **[SDK Quick Start Guide](https://bbvch-ai.github.io/swiss-ai-hub/docs/3_sdk/1_quick_start/)**.
- **To Deploy the Platform:** If you just want to run the platform locally or on-prem to try it out, follow the
  **[Platform Deployment Guide](https://bbvch-ai.github.io/swiss-ai-hub/docs/2_platform/3_deployment_guide/)**.

### Step 4: Develop and Create Your PR

We have a detailed guide on our Git workflow, branching strategy, and code conventions. Please read it carefully.

**➡️
[Full details are in our Ecosystem Contributing Guide](https://bbvch-ai.github.io/swiss-ai-hub/docs/4_ecosystem/1_contributing/)**

Here is the short version:

1. Create a feature branch from `main`.
2. **IMMEDIATELY** open a **Draft Pull Request** from your branch to `main`. This is critical for us to track what's in
   progress and avoid conflicts.
3. Write your code, following our conventions (like Conventional Commits).
4. When your PR is ready, mark it as "Ready for Review".

## 🎨 Other Ways to Contribute

### Documentation

Our documentation in the `docs` folder is always a work in progress. If you find a typo, an unclear explanation, or
a missing guide, a PR to improve it is one of the most valuable contributions you can make.

### Marketing & Branding

We have separate repositories for our website and our `slidev`-based sales/marketing slides. If you have skills in
design, marketing, or communication, you can help us tailor our message to different industries, business areas, or
technical/non-technical audiences.

### Advocacy

- **Be our champion:** Propose a sovereign AI strategy to your company.
- **Showcase our work:** Demonstrate the Swiss AI-Hub to your colleagues and leadership. Your internal support helps
  grow the entire ecosystem.
